FAQ

Common questions

How long until I get my videos?

Standard delivery is 10 to 15 business days after the shoot.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package includes 2 rounds of revisions. Extra rounds can be added and are quoted.

Do monthly plans include photos?

Yes. Monthly plans include 10 to 40 edited photos, depending on the plan, shot in the same sessions as your videos.

Can I just book a shoot day?

Yes. A half day (up to 4 hours) starts at $900 and a full day (up to 8 hours) at $1,600. Editing is $125 an hour.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel is quoted based on where the shoot is.

Do you shoot weddings and real estate?

Yes, on the photo side: headshots, real estate, events and weddings. Ask for a quote.
