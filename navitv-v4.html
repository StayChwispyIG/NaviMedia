The guy behind the visor

I once held a rifle. Now I hold a camera.

I’m Eduardo Navarro. Most people know me as Navi.

I’m a U.S. Army veteran, motorcycle creator, and photographer based in the Inland Empire, California. The gold Road Glide is a big part of the story. So are the friends, shops, and riders I meet along the way.

From a day with the crew at Full Throttle Moto to the next track session, I’m here to keep learning, bring people together, and tell the stories that deserve to be seen.

FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Final files are delivered in 10 to 15 business days.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds. Extra rounds are quoted.

Do monthly plans include photos?

Yes. Plans include 10 to 40 edited photos, depending on the plan.

Can I book a shoot day without a package?

Yes. A half day is $900 and a full day is $1,600. Extra hours are $225 each, and editing is $125 an hour.

Do you travel?

Yes. Travel is quoted based on the location.

Can I add drone footage or a second shooter?

Yes. Both are quoted per project.

What photo shoots do you do?

Headshots, real estate, events and weddings. Each one is quoted.

How do I book?

Pick a date, a package and a place in the booking calendar above, then pay a small deposit to hold the date. You can also send the form below or a DM to @navitv_ on Instagram.
